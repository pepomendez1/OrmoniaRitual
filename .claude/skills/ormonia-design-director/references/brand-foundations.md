# ORMONIA · Brand Foundations

## Core idea

**Lo que cambia adentro se expresa afuera.**

ORMONIA observes change rather than promising control over it.

The brand is about:
- observing;
- accompanying;
- recognizing;
- adapting;
- ritual;
- presence;
- skin behavior;
- rhythm.

## Voice

Editorial, organic, calm, sophisticated, intimate, premium, sensory and informed.

It should never sound:
- mystical for the sake of mysticism;
- pseudo-scientific;
- over-medicalized;
- aggressively sales-driven;
- like generic wellness copy.

## Preferred vocabulary

- acompañar
- observar
- reconocer
- escuchar
- ritmo
- ciclo
- barrera cutánea
- microbioma
- presencia
- umbral
- repliegue
- florecimiento
- restauración
- claridad

Use only when context supports them.

## Avoid

Do not use claims such as:
- equilibrar hormonas
- regular hormonas
- detox
- sanar hormonalmente
- curar
- milagro
- resultados garantizados
- energía femenina sagrada
- diosa
- vibracional
- diagnostic language unless the actual system supports it

## Product philosophy

The four serums have phase references, but the phase is SECONDARY context.

They are not restricted to one menstrual phase.

Users may buy individual serums according to current skin needs.

Do not imply that someone must:
- menstruate;
- track a cycle;
- identify strongly with cyclical living;
- use a serum only during one phase.

## El Ritual

Customer-facing name:
**El Ritual**

Do not show “Pack x4” to customers.

Descriptor:
**Las 4 fases, un solo ritual.**

Price:
ARS 160.000.

The value is NOT “four products at a discount”.

The value is:
- a complete system;
- observing change;
- having four formulas available;
- creating a ritual;
- accompanying different skin moments.

Internally, historical identifiers such as `pack-x4` may remain if technically useful.
