# ORMONIA · Design Principles

## 1. Photography + product + silence

The website should not feel constantly “designed”.

Use:
- real photography;
- product;
- typography;
- white/ivory space;
- quiet transitions.

Avoid filling every viewport with UI.

## 2. Editorial, not magazine-template

Editorial means:
- composition;
- crop;
- rhythm;
- contrast;
- unexpected scale;
- restraint.

Editorial does NOT mean:
- italic serif everywhere;
- uppercase eyebrow everywhere;
- monospace metadata everywhere;
- oversized quote blocks everywhere.

## 3. Controlled asymmetry

Perfect symmetry often makes AI-generated layouts feel generic.

Use asymmetry where natural:
- offset photography;
- uneven image/text proportions;
- intentional negative space;
- different section rhythms.

Do not manufacture asymmetry just to look artistic.

## 4. Section rhythm

Not every section should be 100vh.

Not every section needs:
- eyebrow;
- headline;
- paragraph;
- CTA.

Vary density.

Examples:
- Hero: immersive.
- El Ritual: commercial/editorial.
- Product rail: dense and useful.
- Calendar: object-led.
- El Ciclo: cinematic.
- Test: campaign entry.
- Lecturas: editorial.
- Closing: quiet.

## 5. Cuts are allowed

Clean cuts between sections are valid.

Do not use giant gradients to hide transitions.

Continuity comes from:
- photography;
- typography;
- color;
- rhythm;
- motion.

## 6. Cards should earn their existence

Use a card only if the information benefits from containment.

Do not put:
- manifestos;
- simple copy;
- one image and one line;
inside rounded cards by default.

## 7. Rounded corners

Do not standardize the entire site around one large radius.

Product cards may have a strong radius.

Editorial images can be:
- square;
- lightly rounded;
- full bleed;
depending on composition.

## 8. Buttons

Buttons should feel functional and quiet.

Primary:
clear and direct.

Secondary:
lighter visual weight.

Avoid:
- decorative arrows on every CTA;
- oversized pill buttons;
- excessive hover choreography.

## 9. Product is central

Whenever a product is on screen, ensure:
- label readability where intended;
- clean visual hierarchy;
- no excessive competing decoration.

## 10. One strong idea per section

A section should have one main visual idea.

If the section already has:
- dramatic photography,
do not add:
- huge typography + complex motion + decorative graphics.

If the section already has:
- strong scroll choreography,
keep the content visually restrained.
