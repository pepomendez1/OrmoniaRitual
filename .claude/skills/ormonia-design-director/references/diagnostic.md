# ORMONIA · Test de Perfil de Piel

## Role

This is a real product-discovery / skin-understanding experience.

It is not:
- a medical diagnosis;
- a dermatology tool;
- a hormonal diagnostic;
- a fake AI analysis.

## Visible naming

Preferred Home naming:

Eyebrow:
**TEST DE PERFIL DE PIEL**

Headline:
**Conocé cómo responde tu piel.**

Supporting copy:
**Un recorrido breve para reconocer su comportamiento y lo que necesita hoy.**

CTA:
**HACER EL TEST**

Route can remain:
`/descubri-tu-piel`

## Architecture

Dedicated route.

One question per screen.

Split visual/question layout where appropriate.

Progress.

Answer options/chips.

Previous/Next.

Optional “why we ask” explanation.

Email step.

Result page.

## Current V1 questions

1. Qué te gustaría mejorar hoy en tu piel.
2. Algo más que quieras acompañar.
3. Cómo se siente al final del día.
4. Cómo se manifiesta la incomodidad.
5. Reacción a productos nuevos.
6. Qué llama la atención al observarla de cerca.
7. Cambios durante el mes.
8. Rutina actual.

## Important

Question 7 is complementary.

Do not make menstrual cyclicity a requirement.

## Result architecture

Distinguish:

**Phenotype**
relatively structural behavior.

vs.

**Current state**
what the skin seems to need now.

Final phenotype names/scoring are NOT defined yet.

Do not invent:
- phenotype taxonomy;
- medical axes;
- scoring rules;
- product prescription.

UI may be built provisionally.

## Photography

Close-up real skin photography is preferred for the Home entry.

Do not use generic nature photography when a real skin image is available.

## Interaction typography

Functional text should remain highly legible.

Do not use decorative serif/italic fonts for:
- answers;
- buttons;
- chips;
- progress;
- helper text.
