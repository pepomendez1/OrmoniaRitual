## Hero

Approved core:
- horse/meadow visual direction;
- claim: “Lo que cambia adentro se expresa afuera.”
- CTA related to El Ritual;
- transparent smart header;
- shipping bar only at top.

Hero should not become a carousel UI.

If photography rotates:
- max 2–3 states;
- slow crossfade;
- no dots;
- no arrows;
- no changing copy.
