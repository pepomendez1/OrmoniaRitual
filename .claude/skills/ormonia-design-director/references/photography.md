# ORMONIA · Photography Direction

## Photography is a primary identity asset

Real production photography should generally beat:
- AI placeholders;
- generic tonal placeholders;
- generated beauty backgrounds,
except where an approved non-photographic system has a specific role.

## Production folder

Current production photographs are expected in:

`/public/fotos-editadas/`

Web-optimized derivatives may live in:

`/public/fotos-editadas/web/`

Keep originals intact during development.

Do not ship multi-gigabyte originals in production.

## Photography language

The strongest ORMONIA photography includes:
- forest;
- water;
- rock;
- skin;
- texture;
- movement;
- imperfect grain;
- unusual styling;
- product integrated into nature;
- women inhabiting the landscape rather than posing like standard skincare models.

Preserve the strangeness.

Do not sanitize the campaign into generic beauty photography.

## Use photography as moments

Avoid huge photo mosaics.

Prefer:
- one strong full-width image;
- one model/product hover;
- one close-up;
- one quiet landscape;
- one macro texture.

## Cropping

Never use `object-position: center` globally.

Every important photograph may define:
- desktop crop;
- mobile crop.

Protect:
- faces;
- hands;
- product labels;
- intentional negative space.

## Hover photography

Product cards may use campaign photography on hover.

This is allowed even when the image is not a literal scientific representation of that serum.

Do not write copy that falsely implies:
model X = biological phase X.

## El Ciclo

The approved four material/ingredient-inspired backgrounds belong specifically to El Ciclo.

Do not replace them with campaign photography unless explicitly requested.

El Ciclo should remain a distinct visual system.

## Overlays

Avoid global dark overlays.

Solve legibility in this order:

1. Better crop.
2. Better text position.
3. Correct text tone.
4. Very localized subtle treatment.
5. Overlay only if truly necessary.

## Performance

Do not preload the entire campaign.

Above-the-fold:
load intentionally.

Below-the-fold:
lazy load.

PDP galleries:
only load on their route.
