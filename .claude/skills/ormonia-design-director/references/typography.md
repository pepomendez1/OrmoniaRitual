# ORMONIA · Typography

## Critical rule: ORMONIA wordmark

The ORMONIA logo seen on the physical serum packaging is a WORDMARK / BRAND ASSET.

Do not recreate it with a webfont.

Use the official vector/image asset consistently in:
- Header;
- Footer;
- brand moments where the logo appears.

Never fake it using:
- ALT Systema;
- Cardone;
- Yorick;
- tracking tricks.

## Available typefaces

### ALT Systema Regular
Primary functional sans.

Use for:
- body;
- descriptions;
- PDP copy;
- forms;
- cart;
- account;
- search;
- FAQs;
- functional metadata.

### ALT Systema Semibold
Functional emphasis.

Use for:
- navigation;
- buttons;
- labels;
- eyebrows when actually needed;
- prices / product metadata where appropriate.

### Cardone Trial Light Italic
Editorial accent.

It visually relates to the script/italic language used for product names.

Use for:
- serum names;
- short editorial phrases;
- limited brand moments.

Do NOT automatically use it for:
- every large headline;
- Hero;
- long body;
- buttons;
- navigation.

Do not fake missing weights/styles.

### Yorick Trial Regular
Technical/editorial accent.

It visually relates to the packaging's technical/typewriter-like language.

Use sparingly for:
- ingredients;
- numbers;
- indexes;
- technical product details;
- selected editorial metadata.

Do not use for:
- navigation;
- body paragraphs;
- buttons;
- giant headlines.

## Existing editorial serif

If the previous Hero/editorial serif looked better than Cardone at display scale, it may remain.

Do not replace typography solely because a brand font exists.

Brand fit beats “all fonts must be new”.

## Hierarchy

A page does NOT need all three font families.

Typical combinations:

### Functional ecommerce
ALT Systema + official wordmark.

### Serum PDP
ALT Systema + Cardone for product name + optional Yorick for ingredients.

### Editorial
Existing display serif or controlled Cardone + ALT Systema.

### Diagnostic
Display serif for large headline if approved + ALT Systema for all interaction.

## Tracking

Do not use exaggerated letter-spacing to imitate the logo.

Uppercase labels may use subtle tracking.

## Mobile

Check:
- line wrapping;
- headline line count;
- button widths;
- long product names.

Do not create a separate visual language on mobile.
